## 🌟 Overview & Problem Statement

Every student has experienced the dread of opening textbooks before exam week with zero clue how to allocate their study hours. Most students either:
1. **Fall into Panic & Procrastination:** Intimidated by the sheer volume of syllabus material.
2. **Imbalanced Revision:** Waste 70% of their time on easy topics they already know while neglecting hard subjects that cost them grades.
3. **Passive Cramming:** Re-read notes passively, only to forget 80% within 48 hours (the Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve).

**PadhaiPlan AI** is an intelligent academic revision engine and gamified study station engineered by **Samarth Mishra** to solve this once and for all. It automatically calculates optimal difficulty-weighted study slots, sequences them using spaced-repetition interleaving, powers distraction-free Pomodoro sprints against an RPG Boss, synthesizes full mock exams, and speaks daily tactical briefings aloud.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🧠 Difficulty-Weighted Spaced Repetition Algorithm
* **Strategic Time Balancing:** Hard topics (3.5x weight) automatically receive exponentially more review blocks than Medium (2.0x) or Easy (1.0x) topics.
* **Interleaving Distribution:** Alternates subjects across days to eliminate cognitive fatigue.
* **Final-Day Mastery Capstone:** The day before the exam automatically transitions into a dedicated Mock Problem and Formula Recall sprint.

### 2. ⚔️ Gamified RPG Exam Boss Battle
* **The Procrastination Demon (Level 12):** Features an interactive HP health bar (1,000 HP).
* Each completed 25-minute Pomodoro block triggers an attack sequence dealing **150–200 Damage** to the Boss, unlocking the *Legendary A+ Scholar Trophy* upon victory.

### 3. 🎙️ Two-Way Web Speech AI & Voice Commander
* **Voice Briefing:** Click `🎙️ Voice Briefing` to hear synthesized speech dictate your daily study priorities and motivational strategy.
* **Voice Commander:** Speak commands (*"Start timer"*, *"Quiz me"*, *"Mock exam"*, *"Give advice"*) to navigate hands-free using browser speech recognition.

### 4. 🦉 Interactive Study Buddy ("Byte the Cyber Owl")
* An animated virtual companion who provides study tips, gains XP with completed blocks, and rewards high-fives with particle confetti.

### 5. 📉 Ebbinghaus Memory Retention Curve Simulator
* An interactive Canvas graph that visually contrasts passive cramming (memory drops to 20% in 4 days) with PadhaiPlan's Spaced Repetition (consistently maintains 92%+ long-term retention).

### 6. 🔮 Exam Grade Predictor & Panic Index Meter
* Real-time sliders calculate your expected score (e.g., *94% A Grade*) and categorize your Panic Level (*Calm & Ready*, *Moderate*, or *High Alert Sprint*) with actionable turnaround advice.

### 7. 📝 Full Timed Mock Exam Simulator
* Generates full-length multi-subject practice papers on demand, calculates real-time percentage scores, and provides diagnostic feedback.

### 8. 🎧 100% Offline Synthesized Web Audio Station
* Synthesizes real-time audio in the browser with zero external CDN dependencies:
  * 🌧️ *Rain & Thunderstorm*
  * 🧠 *Alpha Waves (40Hz Focus Binaural Beat)*
  * ☕ *Cozy Study Cafe White Noise*

### 9. 📦 1-Click Calendar Sync (.ICS) & Desk Print
* **Export to Calendar:** Downloads a standard `.ics` file to import study blocks directly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
* **Print Desk Sheet:** Optimized printable layout (`@media print`) for pinning physical schedules on study desks.

### 10. 🌐 Bilingual & 3 Visual Themes
* **Languages:** English (Default for global judges) ⇄ Hinglish/Hindi.
* **Themes:** Cyber Dark, Clean Light, and Cozy Sunset.
